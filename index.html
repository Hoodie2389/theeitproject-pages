Our Mission

The EIT Project was founded with three words in mind - Empower, Inspire, Together.

We aim provide a platform for young, aspiring musicians to make an impact on their community through musical enrichment and the promotion of local arts and talent. Our goal is to create a vibrant space where the next generation of performers can grow — and where music brings people together.
