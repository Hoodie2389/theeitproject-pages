Our Story

Music is a catalyst for happiness.

Based in Singapore, The EIT Project brings people together through music enrichment, community programmes, performances, and events. It was founded on the belief that music is a catalyst for happiness and enrichment in life. Our founder realised that through her musicianship, she was able to find a creative outlet and a way to express her emotions to the outside world — bringing her immense joy and a sense of fulfillment, from both the confidence her instrument gave her and the happiness it brought to those around her. Now she strives to spread the happiness and enrichment that music brought her, to the community.

We hope that through the EIT Project we inspire and spread enrichment, happiness, and fulfillment to all who encounter music.
