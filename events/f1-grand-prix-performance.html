Sign Up for a Chance to Perform at the F1 Singapore Grand Prix

Sat, 05 Sept 2026 2:00 pm – 9:50 pm Timbre+ One North
Back to Initiatives

Singapore's next breakout band could be YOU — one winning act walks away with a stage at the F1 Singapore Grand Prix, plus slots at Music Matters Live and All That Matters. Applications for Youth Music Matters close Sat 23rd August — apply now and make your big dreams real.

🎤 The EIT Project is proud to be YMM's Community Partner 🎤

Youth Music Matters is back. Singapore, are you ready? Youth Music Matters returns for its 3rd edition — and this year, the stakes just got bigger. One winning band will take the stage at the F1 Singapore Airlines Singapore Grand Prix. Yes, THAT stage. Alongside a slot at Music Matters Live at CQ @ Clarke Quay and a spot at All That Matters. This isn't just a competition. It's your shot at becoming Singapore's next breakout act. 🎤

Applications are open NOW.
