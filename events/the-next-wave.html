The EIT Project presents to you, The Next Wave! A partnership with Timbre+ One North, a monthly series featuring talented musicians across Singapore. If you want to take part, please email: ciara.mengyao.zhang@gmail.com!

About the initiative

Through this initiative, selected student performers will have the opportunity to take the stage at Timbre+ One-North, performing short live sets in a professional venue setting before a public audience. Each edition will feature 3–4 acts from different schools, across genres such as rock, jazz, classical, acoustic, and vocal.

This platform is designed to give students:

  • Real-world performance experience in a professional venue
  • Exposure beyond the school environment
  • Opportunities to connect with a wider community of young musicians
